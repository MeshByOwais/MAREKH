Time settings
Scenario assumptions · simulated data

Editable demonstration assumptions. Ground temperature varies in synthetic 100 m cells and over a 24-hour scenario clock. Pressure is regional context. Radiation uses a historical Gale Crater reference, not a Columbia Hills measurement. Alert thresholds are yours, not NASA suit limits.

Walking model

Walking model: seconds = 3D distance ÷ level speed × (1 + slowdown × |rise/run|). It is an editable planning estimate, not a validated astronaut mobility model. At the stop, Add 5 min records sampling time. The return forecast includes at least the planned stop; skipping never removes time already spent. Animation speed never changes dose or walking time.

References

Context: NASA pressure reference · Historical Curiosity RAD dose equivalent. The rate is µSv/h; it is not absorbed dose in µGy.

Science stop / historical observation

Gertrude Weise: a clue left by water.

Spirit’s wheel exposed silica-rich soil east of Home Plate. NASA describes hot springs or volcanic steam as possible origins; both involve water. This observation provides context for the proposed science approach, whose mapped location remains approximate.
