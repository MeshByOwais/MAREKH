A location supported by evidence

Spirit observed the silica-rich soil named Gertrude Weise east of Home Plate. The amber area comes from registering the location marker in the published research map to this orthophoto.

The 15 m buffer is a local planning allowance. Absolute map accuracy is unknown. The approach and staging ground still require slope, obstacle and route checks.

Two different observations

The orbital image was acquired on 22 November 2006. Spirit’s science mosaic was acquired on 21 May 2007, sol 1202. The orbital image provides terrain context; it does not show the later wheel excavation.

Projection checked

The detached PDS label supplies the intended Mars projection and aligns exactly with the DTM grid. The JP2’s embedded georeferencing differs and is not used.

Coverage, beyond the crop

Keep the boundaries visible.

The cyan outline follows the shared valid elevation and image footprint. The amber square is the roughly 1 km mission crop. Missing data outside that footprint stays unknown.

The hillshade is derived from measured elevation at a 1:1 horizontal and vertical scale. Orbital imagery retains its grayscale red-band appearance.
